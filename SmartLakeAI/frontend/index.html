🎯 Multi-Object Segmentation

Detects and outlines precise polygon segmentation masks and bounding boxes for multiple floating waste items simultaneously in high-resolution video streams.

🏷️ Material Classification

Classifies objects into Plastic, Metal, Paper, Wood, Rubber, Fabric, Fishing Gear, and distinguishes waste from natural marine life like Fish and Plants.

📊 Pollution Level Density

Calculates real-time AI-based visual waste density indicators (Low, Moderate, High, Critical) with persistent multi-object tracking to prevent duplicate counts.

🤖 Autonomous Cleaning Robot Integration

SmartLake AI is designed to integrate directly with lake-cleaning robots and autonomous collection boats. By exporting live object coordinates, material classifications, and waste density maps, autonomous vessels can navigate directly to high-density pollution zones for targeted cleanup.
